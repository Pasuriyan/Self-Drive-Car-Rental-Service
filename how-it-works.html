Simple Requirements

What You Need to Rent With Us

We believe in making self-drive accessible without archaic paperwork.

  • Valid Driver's License: Must be valid and held for at least 12 months. Non-US visitors can upload their national license + IDP.
  • Minimum Age 21: Drivers aged 21-24 have access to all standard sedans and SUVs with zero underage penalty surcharges.
  • Credit or Debit Card: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Apple Pay. With verified KYC, zero security deposit is held.
  • Smartphone with Bluetooth: iOS or Android device required to pair with the car's Bluetooth beacon for door locking and unlocking.
